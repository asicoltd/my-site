Biography & Story

About Me

I graduated in Computer Science & Engineering from East West University in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My primary drive is engineering intelligent software systems that solve concrete problems in clinical diagnosis, communications, and decentralized networks.

In academic research, I have focused on applied computer vision for early clinical diagnosis—co-authoring three Springer LNNS papers on detecting childhood Hand, Foot, and Mouth Disease, identifying gastrointestinal lesions from endoscopy imagery, and automated rose plant pathology using YOLO.

Beyond deep learning, I build resilient networking tools. I experiment with Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN) routing protocols for intermittent environments and build decentralized applications.

Peer-Reviewed Work

Research Publications

Published three peer-reviewed conference papers in Springer’s Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems (LNNS).

Springer LNNS DMIS 2024 DOI: 10.1007/978-981-96-6063-6_25

Ensemble Deep Learning for Enhanced Detection and Classification of Hand, Foot, and Mouth Disease

Md Asif Hasan and Collaborators

International Conference on Data Mining and Information Security (DMIS 2024) • Springer, Singapore

Addresses the clinical challenge of accurately identifying Hand, Foot, and Mouth Disease (HFMD) in young children. By training an ensemble of convolutional backbones, the framework mitigates individual model variance and delivers robust diagnostic precision on dermatological lesion images.

View on Springer
Springer LNNS DMIS 2024 DOI: 10.1007/978-981-96-6066-7_32

Classification of Peptic Ulcer and Digestive Tract Disorders Using Endoscopic Images

Md Asif Hasan and Collaborators

International Conference on Data Mining and Information Security (DMIS 2024) • Springer, Singapore

Diagnosing gastrointestinal tract disorders from endoscopic imagery requires high visual discrimination across subtle mucosal changes. This study applies transfer learning across endoscopic datasets to categorize ulcerations and lesions, assisting medical professionals with automated triage.

View on Springer
Springer LNNS ICTCS 2024 DOI: 10.1007/978-981-96-5607-3_13

Rose Disease Detection Using YOLO: A Deep Learning-Based Approach

Md Asif Hasan and Collaborators

9th International Conference on ICT for Sustainable Development (ICTCS 2024) • Springer, Singapore

Automates the visual identification of common rose plant pathologies in agricultural cultivation. Evaluates real-time single-stage object detectors (YOLO) to provide fast, localized bounding box detection of foliar infections under variable field lighting conditions.

View on Springer
