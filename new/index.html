01 / Background

About Me

A researcher who builds and an engineer who investigates.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from East West University with a focus on solving complex computational challenges at the intersection of AI, networking, and distributed systems.

My research encompasses explainable AI (XAI) for cybersecurity, real-time computer vision pipelines for embedded systems, deep reinforcement learning for high-frequency algorithmic decision-making, and delay-tolerant routing protocols for intermittently connected networks.

I believe in rigorous empirical validation combined with clean, accessible engineering. When I am not training models or optimizing backpropagation pipelines, I design interactive simulations, write open-source tools, and mentor students in competitive programming.

🔬 Interpretable Machine Learning

Developing SHAP and LIME-based explainable AI frameworks for IoT intrusion detection and security-critical systems.

👁️ Applied Computer Vision

Constructing real-time detection, tracking, and assistive vision algorithms deployed on edge hardware and embedded robotics.

🌐 Distributed & Delay-Tolerant Networks

Benchmarking buffer management, cache replacement, and message replication in intermittently connected mobile ad-hoc networks.

⚡ High-Throughput Engineering
