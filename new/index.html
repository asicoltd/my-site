01 / Background

Architecting Empirical AI Systems

Bridging theoretical machine learning with high-throughput production infrastructure.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from East West University with a rigorous focus on solving complex computational challenges where machine learning intersects with distributed architectures and network security.

My scholarly work investigates explainable AI (XAI) for IoT intrusion detection, real-time edge computer vision pipelines, deep reinforcement learning for dynamic market execution, and opportunistic delay-tolerant routing protocols across partitioned networks.

I prioritize deep technical craftsmanship: empirical validation over vague assertions, clean code over artificial complexity, and verifiable benchmarks over hype. When away from the terminal, I construct algorithmic simulations and mentor students in competitive programming.
